Privacy Policy - AntCashManager

Last updated: May 16, 2026

Summary: AntCashManager does not collect personal or sensitive data through custom analytics. Only app usage data is collected (usage-only analytics).

1. Data we collect

The app records only technical and usage-statistics events (for example screen openings and functional actions), in order to improve stability and usability.

2. Data we do NOT collect

  • User free-text content (queries, notes, titles, descriptions)
  • Sensitive/content transaction fields (payee, location, tags, descriptive amount details)
  • Email addresses or direct personal identifiers
  • Raw error messages, stack traces, or content that may include PII

3. Allowed events (usage-only analytics)

  • screen_view (Firebase standard event)
  • transactions_filter_applied
  • transactions_filter_cleared
  • transaction_add_opened
  • receipt_scan_opened
  • transaction_form_opened
  • transaction_form_cancelled
  • transaction_submit_success
  • backup_create_requested
  • backup_file_saved
  • backup_file_save_error
  • restore_open_requested
  • restore_file_selected
  • delete_all_data_confirmed
  • reset_preferences_confirmed

4. Purpose

Usage data is used only for aggregated product analytics, technical monitoring, and user experience improvements.

5. Policy updates

This page may be updated if analytics events or privacy policy rules change. Changes are versioned in the project repository.
