AntCashManager is a simple, privacy-focused personal finance management app for Android. 
Take control of your money with an intuitive interface that makes tracking expenses and budgets effortless.

🎯 KEY FEATURES

📊 Transaction Tracking
• Record income and expenses quickly with easy-to-use forms
• Categorize transactions (Food, Transport, Bills, Entertainment, etc.)
• View detailed transaction history with search and filtering
• Attach receipts via camera/gallery for visual records

💰 Budget Management
• Set monthly budgets per category
• Real-time progress tracking against your budget limits
• Visual alerts when approaching or exceeding limits
• Historical budget analysis and trends

📈 Charts & Analytics
• Visual breakdown of expenses by category (pie charts, bar charts)
• Monthly and yearly spending trends
• Income vs Expense comparisons
• Custom date range analysis

🛡️ Privacy & Security
• All data stored locally on your device
• No account signup required
• No tracking or ads
• Open source - full transparency on code

📱 User Experience
• Clean, modern Material Design interface
• Multi-language support (5 languages: English, Italian, German, French, Spanish)
• Dark mode support
• Smooth animations and intuitive navigation

💾 Data Management
• Export transactions to CSV/JSON
• Backup and restore functionality
• Easy data import from other finance apps
• Complete data ownership - delete anytime
